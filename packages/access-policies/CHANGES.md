# Changes from today's permission model

Every cell of the 26 × 6 capability matrix (`packages/shared/src/capabilities.ts`) is reproduced
by the default grants, and `tests/matrix.test.mjs` proves it. Where the Cedar model **deliberately**
differs, the change is listed here with its reason and its test. P11.3 carries this file into
`packages/access-policies/CHANGES.md`, and the owner's approval of this list at G1 is the approval
P11.3 asks for.

| #   | Change                                                                                                                                                  | Who gains or loses                                                                                | Why                                                                    | Test                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| C1  | Completing a briefing slot needs you to be its assigned briefer                                                                                         | everyone loses completing others' or unassigned slots                                             | F03-016: the rule was inverted                                         | `changes.test.mjs` C1 |
| C2  | Acknowledging an announcement needs you in its audience                                                                                                 | people outside the audience lose it                                                               | F04-005: the acknowledgement returned the announcement to anyone       | C2                    |
| C3  | An IC reads the roster (with phone numbers) of their assigned stations only                                                                             | IC loses other stations' rosters                                                                  | F04-004                                                                | C3                    |
| C4  | An IC sends station announcements to their assigned stations only                                                                                       | IC loses other stations                                                                           | F04-024                                                                | C4                    |
| C5  | Changing a role is `People.AssignRole`, separate from `Roster.Edit`, and nobody grants a role at or above their own; only platform admins create Admins | Deputy loses setting roles through the import; Chief and event Admin lose granting their own rank | F03-001: a Deputy could make themselves Admin                          | C5                    |
| C6  | Lost-and-found close-out is `LostFound.CloseOut` (Deputy, Chief, Admin)                                                                                 | Lead loses it (was `report.generate`)                                                             | F02/F03 matrix oddity: the Lead could close out items they may not log | C6                    |
| C7  | Listing stations and days is `Structure.Read` (IC, Deputy, Chief, Lead, Admin)                                                                          | IC and Lead gain listing stations; IC gains listing days                                          | matrix oddity: days needed `user.read` and stations `config.manage`    | C7                    |
| C8  | Printing a card batch is `Card.GenerateBatch` (Configure)                                                                                               | no cell changes (Chief, Admin)                                                                    | matrix oddity: it needed `user.provision`                              | matrix port           |
| C9  | Security and privacy settings (`Settings.ManageSecurity`, `Settings.ManagePrivacy`) are platform-admin only                                             | Chief and event Admin lose `lostPersonPurgeHours` and the other privacy keys                      | F01 § settings: "Admin (privacy)", "Admin (security)"; ADR-003 classes | C9                    |
| C10 | The attendance root is a membership flag set in event settings                                                                                          | no role change                                                                                    | F02-017: it was an env email                                           | `attendance codes…`   |
| C11 | Capture is allowed in `REHEARSAL`, `LIVE`, and `CLOSED` with late sync only                                                                             | nobody captures in `DRAFT`, `READY` or `ARCHIVED`                                                 | ADR-004 lifecycle; replaces `SHIFT_HOURS_ALWAYS_OPEN`                  | C11                   |
| C12 | `own.read` becomes seven self-service actions with ownership conditions                                                                                 | no one gains; C1 and C2 are the only losses                                                       | `own.read` gated writes (F02 § P02.9)                                  | `check-in…`, C1, C2   |
