   typecheck, `arch:check` (Node 24 only), `check:hardcoding`, `prisma migrate deploy` on
   `spoh2027_test`, shared, access-policies, CDK, server and client coverage suites, and
   `check:coverage`. Today this caught a `check:hardcoding` failure (a `spoh.` prefix in a
   `Symbol.for` key) that would have stopped CI.
3. After a push, watch CI and the staging deploy to the end. A failed deploy or smoke is fixed
   before the next batch.
4. No soaks, shadow releases, per-feature staging acceptance, full browser or visual runs, or
   release-plan approvals. Write browser specs with the features; P16 runs them.
5. A step is **done** when its code and tests are green in CI and the pipeline has deployed it.
   Put any staging, soak, load or owner-sign-off part of its _Done when_ into P16.8 (append the
   line to `phases/P16-verification.md` § P16.8) and close the step with a note saying so.
6. Ask the owner only about cost, production, unrecoverable data and product rules the ADRs leave
   open. Otherwise take the recommendation and record it as **assumed** in `DECISIONS.md`.
7. When every feature step is closed, start P16 in ADR-011 §6's order.
