# Report and export event-day repair

The deployed `72202ae76a8285e3a2f072ab274f95f792bb7dc5` accepted `eventDayId` on report summary and export requests but ignored it. A nonexistent day returned HTTP 200. The retained staging fixture held captures on only one day, so its matching selected/all-days totals did not establish filtering. The reproduction is preserved in `.local/p16-report-day-live-red-v1-20261022.json`.

Resolve an event-owned day through the report's existing repeatable-read transaction. Apply the event timezone and operational boundary, intersect explicit bounds, expose the effective range, and refuse missing/foreign days. Use that effective range for the separate visitor-values export sheet. Select daily attendance by its calendar-day identity rather than comparing a UTC calendar-date anchor to operational instants. Empty intersections have no attendance or fallback overlap. Omitted day and existing all-days behavior remain compatible; gift inventory and active-member counts retain their current-state meaning.

Seven unit regressions cover the generator, transaction-scoped lookup, missing day, omitted bounds, intersection and DST. Two generator regressions were actually run before the fix and failed with native exit 1 (`p16-report-day-red-v2-native-20261022.exit.json`). The preceding v1 controller failed to locate its configuration and is retained separately.

Ten new PostgreSQL integration cases distinguish records immediately before, at and inside the day and at its exclusive end. They cover Singapore operational boundaries, London DST, New York attendance, explicit and empty intersections, all-days compatibility, missing/foreign-day refusal for summary/CSV/XLSX, and exclusion of other-day visitor values from both export formats. These cases require the existing isolated CI PostgreSQL service; writing them does not claim they passed.

The original eleven-file summary repair remains byte-for-byte preserved. No schema, identity, lifecycle, policy, infrastructure, CI, audit exception or coverage-floor change is part of this repair.
